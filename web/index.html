Open a window and it tiles itself.

Tiling that just works

No SIP changes. No shell scripts.

A yabai-style BSP engine tiles your windows automatically, with virtual workspaces and a scriptable CLI — all native, all without disabling System Integrity Protection.

▦

Automatic tiling

A binary space partitioning engine tiles windows as you open them. Focus, swap, resize, zoom, and rotate with vim-style keys.

🗂️

Virtual workspaces

Group apps into workspaces and switch with a hotkey, a trackpad swipe, or a jump to the most recent — across multiple displays.

⌨️

Scriptable & native

skhd-style shortcuts, a plain-TOML config that hot-reloads, a CLI for scripting, and native SwiftUI settings.
