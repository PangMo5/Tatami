Workspaces, tiled

A space for every task — tiled automatically.

Group your apps into virtual workspaces and let a yabai-style BSP engine tile their windows for you — all native, with no SIP changes and no shell scripting.

🗂️

Virtual workspaces

Group your apps into workspaces and switch with a hotkey, a trackpad swipe, or a jump to the most recent — across multiple displays.

▦

Automatic tiling

A yabai-style BSP engine tiles each workspace's windows as you open them. Focus, swap, resize, zoom, and rotate with vim-style keys.

⌨️

Scriptable & native

skhd-style shortcuts, a plain-TOML config that hot-reloads, a CLI for scripting, and native SwiftUI settings.
